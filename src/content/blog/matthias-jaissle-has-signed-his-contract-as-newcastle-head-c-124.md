Newcastle United have secured their new head coach in Matthias Jaissle, who has signed a contract with the club until June 2030.  The deal was finalized after Newcastle paid €11m compensation to Al Ahli for his release. 

## Background

Matthias Jaissle had made quite a name for himself during his time at Al Ahli, quickly establishing a reputation as a promising young coach. His tactical acumen and ability to develop players have been widely praised.  Newcastle, meanwhile, are known for their ambitious recruitment strategy, often looking to bring in fresh talent with the potential to make an immediate impact. With recent struggles on the pitch, they clearly see Jaissle as the man to lead them back to success. 

## What's next

With the contract signed, the focus will now likely shift to official announcements from both Newcastle and Al Ahli.  We can expect a formal unveiling of Jaissle as the new head coach in the coming days, along with perhaps some introductory comments from club officials.
