Real Madrid has reportedly reached a verbal agreement on personal terms with forward Yan Diomande.  Following a similar agreement with Paris Saint-Germain in June, Real has secured Diomande's approval for the proposed contract.  Despite an initial bid of €90 million plus €10 million being rejected by RB Leipzig, Real is expected to resubmit a revised offer for the player. Meanwhile, PSG has re-engaged with Leipzig today in an attempt to secure Diomande's transfer.
