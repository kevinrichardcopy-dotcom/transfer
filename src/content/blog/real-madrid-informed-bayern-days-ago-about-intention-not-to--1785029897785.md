Real Madrid has made it clear to Bayern Munich that they have no intention of disrupting their relationship in pursuit of Michael Olise. Despite Florentino Pérez's keen interest in signing the talented player, Real Madrid will only pursue a move for Olise if Bayern Munich expresses willingness to sell.  With Bayern showing no signs of opening doors this summer, Real Madrid has shifted its focus to other targets, including Diomande.
