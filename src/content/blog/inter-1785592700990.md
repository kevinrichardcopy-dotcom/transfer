##  Inter Milan Complete Signing of Defender Alessandro Bastoni 

Italian giants Inter Milan have announced the signing of highly-rated defender Alessandro Bastoni from Serie A rivals Fiorentina. The transfer fee for the young centre-back is reported to be €35 million. No details about the length of Bastoni's contract at San Siro have been released.


## Background

Alessandro Bastoni burst onto the scene with his displays for Atalanta's youth academy before securing a move to Fiorentina in 2017. The young defender has quickly established himself as one of Serie A’s most promising talents, playing in a variety of defensive positions and showcasing both strength and technical ability on the ball. Inter Milan are known for their strong youth development system and have often integrated promising young players into their first team. They are currently rebuilding after several seasons of success under Antonio Conte and will be looking to challenge for major honours once again.  Fiorentina, meanwhile, are a club traditionally known for developing their own talent and selling it on for profit.

## What's Next 

It is expected that Bastoni will undergo a medical examination at Inter Milan before the deal is officially announced. Further details regarding his contract length and squad number are likely to be released in due course. The move could see Bastoni join up with his new teammates for pre-season training shortly,  and he may even feature in some friendly matches as Inter prepare for the upcoming season.
