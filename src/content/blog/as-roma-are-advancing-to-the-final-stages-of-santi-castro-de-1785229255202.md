AS Roma are closing in on the signing of Santi Castro from Bologna.  The Giallorossi have put a €30 million plus €5 million proposal on the table for the young midfielder, with further details regarding add-ons still to be negotiated. 

Castro has reportedly already agreed personal terms with Roma.


## Background

Santi Castro is a highly promising young talent who operates primarily as a central midfielder. While Bologna is his current club, he's been steadily making a name for himself in Serie A with impressive displays of both defensive solidity and creative passing.  Roma, meanwhile, are known for their ambitious transfer strategy, often targeting young players with high potential to develop into key figures for the club.  

AS Roma have been particularly active in bolstering their midfield this season, looking to strengthen a crucial area of the pitch that has seen some inconsistencies in recent years. Bologna, on the other hand, while enjoying a respectable campaign, may be open to selling Castro if they receive a substantial offer, allowing them to reinvest funds elsewhere.

## What's Next

With Castro reportedly already agreeing to join Roma, the next steps will likely involve finalizing the financial details between the two clubs and completing a medical examination for Castro.  Once these hurdles are cleared, an official announcement from AS Roma confirming the transfer should be imminent.
