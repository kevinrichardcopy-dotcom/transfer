It’s official: John Stones is heading to Serie A! The English defender has agreed terms with Inter Milan on a contract that runs until June 2028, with no option for extension.  The deal reportedly sees Stones earn around €4 million net per season. He will undergo a medical in Milan shortly.


## Background

John Stones is a proven Premier League center-back known for his composure on the ball and ability to start attacks from deep. Though he has faced criticism at times for positional awareness, his passing range and technical skill have made him a valuable asset for both club and country. 

Inter Milan are a club with a history of ambitious transfers, often looking to bring in experienced players who can bolster their squad for a serious title challenge. Currently, they are performing well domestically and seeking to make an impact on the European stage.  

## What's Next

With contract terms agreed upon and a medical scheduled, the final stages of Stones’ transfer to Inter appear imminent. It is likely that an official announcement will be made shortly after his successful medical examination in Milan.
