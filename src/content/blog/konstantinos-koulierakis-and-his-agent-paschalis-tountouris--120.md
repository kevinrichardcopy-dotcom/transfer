## Greek Defender Heads to Rome

Greek defender Konstantinos Koulierakis has completed his move from German side Wolfsburg to AS Roma.  The transfer fee is set at €16.5 million with an additional €1.5 million in potential add-ons. The deal also includes a 20% sell-on clause for Wolfsburg, ensuring they benefit from any future sale of the player. Koulierakis and his agent Paschalis Tountouris are currently traveling to Rome to finalize the transfer.

## Background

Koulierakis is known as a strong, physical defender who operates primarily in central defense. While he's still relatively early in his career, he has already gained valuable experience playing for Wolfsburg in the Bundesliga. This move represents a significant step up for Koulierakis, joining a Roma side that consistently competes in European competitions and aims to challenge for top honors domestically. 

Roma have historically been active in the transfer market, often seeking talented young players with potential. Their current squad is undergoing some rebuilding as they look to reassert themselves as one of Serie A's dominant forces.  As for Wolfsburg, this sale suggests a willingness to part with promising assets in order to generate funds for future investments. 

## What's Next?

With the player and his agent already en route to Rome, it seems likely that Koulierakis will undergo a medical examination before officially signing his contract with AS Roma. An official announcement from the club is expected imminently, confirming the transfer details and welcoming the new arrival.
