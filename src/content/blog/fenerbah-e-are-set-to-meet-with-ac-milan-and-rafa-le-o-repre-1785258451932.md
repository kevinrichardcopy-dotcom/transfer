Fenerbahçe are preparing to make their move for in-demand AC Milan winger Rafael Leão. According to reports, the Turkish club will meet with representatives from both Milan and Leão himself to present an official offer. 

However, it seems Fenerbahçe face an uphill battle, as AC Milan owner Gerry Cardinale has reportedly ruled out any possibility of a loan deal for the Portuguese star. The final decision ultimately rests with AC Milan and Leão on whether or not to accept Fenerbahçe's proposal.

## Background

Rafael Leão has established himself as one of Serie A's most exciting forwards. Known for his blistering pace, direct running, and clinical finishing, he has become a key player for AC Milan since joining from Lille in 2019.  Fenerbahçe, meanwhile, are a Turkish giants with a history of competing at the highest level both domestically and in Europe. They have consistently looked to bolster their squad with high-profile signings in recent years as they aim to regain their former glory.

AC Milan, after a period of rebuilding, are now firmly back among Serie A's elite, challenging for major honors once again.  They may be reluctant to part with Leão, particularly given his crucial role in their recent success. 

## What's Next

With Fenerbahçe set to formally table their offer, the coming days will likely see a flurry of activity as negotiations between the two clubs and Leão himself intensify. If Milan are indeed open to selling,  it remains to be seen what kind of fee they would demand for one of their prized assets. 

We can expect more news to emerge soon regarding the progress of these talks. Should an agreement be reached, a medical examination for Leão will likely follow, paving the way for an official announcement and his potential transfer to Fenerbahçe.
