RB Leipzig striker Yan Diomande's future appears set away from the Bundesliga side. The forward did not participate in today's friendly match and will reportedly not even travel with the team to Austria for pre-season camp. 

Sources indicate that Diomande’s transfer to Real Madrid is all but complete, having been finalized last Saturday.  The move is currently awaiting Real Madrid's green light, contingent upon them securing a suitable replacement.


## Background

Diomande is a striker known for his physical presence and aerial prowess. He has shown potential throughout his career, though consistency has remained a challenge. 

RB Leipzig, under the guidance of their sporting director, have cultivated a reputation for identifying and developing young talent. They often employ a high-pressing, attacking style of play that requires energetic forwards like Diomande.  Real Madrid, on the other hand, are one of the most decorated clubs in world football, known for their star-studded squads and relentless pursuit of trophies. The Spanish giants consistently seek to strengthen their attack with top-tier talent.

## What's Next 

With the transfer largely agreed upon, all eyes will now be on Real Madrid’s next move.  They will need to find a suitable replacement for Diomande before officially confirming his arrival at the Santiago Bernabéu stadium. Fans eagerly await any announcements regarding medical checks and the formal unveiling of the transfer.
