Roma have secured their first signing of the summer transfer window, with Santiago Castro set to join from Bologna in a deal worth over €35 million. The midfielder is currently in Rome and expected to finalize his move on Tuesday.

## Background

Castro has established himself as a promising talent in Serie A during his time at Bologna. Known for his creativity and ability to dictate the tempo of a game, he operates primarily as a central midfielder. He's become a key player for the club and has attracted attention from several top teams across Europe. 

AS Roma are known for their proactive approach in the transfer market, often looking to bolster their squad with young, talented players who can contribute immediately. The Giallorossi have been undergoing something of a rebuilding phase recently and Castro's arrival could be a significant step forward.  

## What's next

With Castro already in Rome, the next steps are likely to involve him completing a medical examination and putting pen to paper on a contract with Roma. Once those formalities are taken care of, an official announcement from the club is expected to follow shortly after. The transfer fee of over €35 million highlights the significant investment Roma are making in Castro's potential.
