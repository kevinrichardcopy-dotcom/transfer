In a confirmation of an exclusive report, Inter Milan sporting director Piero Ausilio has publicly acknowledged the club's interest in Tottenham Hotspur defender Cristian Romero.  Ausilio revealed that discussions have taken place with both Romero's agent and Tottenham officials, indicating a serious pursuit of the Argentine center-back. The outcome of these negotiations remains uncertain, with Ausilio stating "we will see what happens."
