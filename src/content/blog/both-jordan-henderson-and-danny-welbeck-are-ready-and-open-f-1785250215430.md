Henderson is expected to depart Brentford at the end of his contract, making him a free agent available to any team. ## Background

Jordan Henderson has long been a prominent figure in English football. Known for his energetic midfield presence and leadership qualities, he captained Liverpool to significant domestic and European success during his time at Anfield. His recent move to Brentford marked a new chapter in his career as he sought fresh challenges. 

Chelsea, under their current ownership, have demonstrated an ambition to attract high-profile players and rebuild their squad following a mixed season. They are often linked with big-name transfers, especially those who bring experience and proven quality. Welbeck has carved out a solid career in the Premier League, known for his clinical finishing and tactical versatility. He has previously played for Arsenal, Manchester United, and Brighton, demonstrating his adaptability to different teams and styles of play.

## What's next

With personal terms reportedly settled, the focus will likely shift to finalizing the transfer agreements between the clubs involved. It wouldn't be surprising to see a swift resolution given the apparent willingness of both Henderson and Welbeck to join Chelsea. 
Once these details are confirmed, we can expect an official announcement from Chelsea, followed by medical examinations for the new signings.
