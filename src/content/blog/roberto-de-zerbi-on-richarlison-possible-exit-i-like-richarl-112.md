Tottenham Hotspur forward Richarlison's future at the club appears increasingly uncertain after manager Roberto de Zerbi hinted that a move away could be on the cards. While praising Richarlison's attitude and behaviour, De Zerbi stated that the team would respect his wishes if he decided to pursue a transfer. 

## Background

Richarlison has been a key player for Tottenham since joining from Everton in the summer of 2022. Known for his direct running and powerful finishing, the Brazilian international is a versatile forward who can operate effectively as a centre-forward or on either wing.  

Tottenham's transfer approach under recent managers has often involved a mix of shrewd signings and high-profile acquisitions. The club typically seeks to strengthen key areas while maintaining a balance between established stars and promising young talent. 

## What's next

Given De Zerbi's comments, it is likely that Tottenham will engage in discussions with any clubs interested in Richarlison. The coming weeks could see official bids being submitted, followed by negotiations between the two clubs. If an agreement is reached, a medical would then need to be completed before the transfer could be officially confirmed.
