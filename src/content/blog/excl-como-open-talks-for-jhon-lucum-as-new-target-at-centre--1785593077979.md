The Colombian defender is currently contracted to Bologna, but could be on the move this summer. Juventus have also expressed interest in Lucumí since June and continue to monitor his situation closely.

## Background

Jhon Lucumí is a Colombian center-back who has been playing professional football for several years. He plays primarily as a central defender, known for his ability to read the game and make crucial interceptions. While Bologna has been his most recent club, he's previously gained experience at other Italian sides. 

Como are looking to bolster their defensive options ahead of the upcoming season. They have a reputation for being a club that actively seeks out promising young talent and develops it within their ranks. Juventus, on the other hand, are consistently among the top teams in Italy and Europe, known for their strong squad depth and ambition to win major titles. Their interest in Lucumí reflects their commitment to finding quality players who can contribute at the highest level.

## What's Next

It remains to be seen whether Como will ultimately secure a deal for Lucumí. Further negotiations are likely to take place between both clubs, as well as with the player himself. If an agreement is reached, there would typically be a medical examination conducted before the transfer is formally announced. Juventus' continued interest adds another layer of complexity to the situation, and they could potentially make a move themselves should Como prove unsuccessful in their pursuit.
