##  Monaco Seal Deal for Young Talent Abline 

AS Monaco have agreed a deal to sign promising midfielder Matthis Abline from Nantes. The transfer is set to cost €25 million plus add-ons, as reported by multiple sources. This young talent's move to the Ligue 1 side represents a significant investment in their future.  

## Background

Matthis Abline is a highly regarded young player known for his midfield prowess. He has steadily climbed the ranks at Nantes and showcased his abilities with impressive performances in recent seasons.  Monaco, on the other hand, are renowned for their talent development and nurturing of young players, often bringing in promising prospects from across Europe. The club's reputation for investing in youth and providing a platform for growth likely played a key role in attracting Abline to the Stade Louis II.

Nantes, meanwhile, have built a strong track record of developing talented players who go on to achieve success elsewhere. They will undoubtedly be looking to replace Abline's contributions with another promising prospect.


## What's Next 

With the deal agreed, Monaco and Nantes will now work towards finalizing the transfer.  The next steps typically involve a medical examination for Abline followed by the official announcement of his move to Monaco. Once these formalities are completed, Abline is expected to join his new teammates ahead of the upcoming season.
