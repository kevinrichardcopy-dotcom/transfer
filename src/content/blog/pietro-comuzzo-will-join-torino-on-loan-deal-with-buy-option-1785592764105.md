Torino have strengthened their defensive options by securing the services of Pietro Comuzzo on loan from Fiorentina. The deal includes a buy option clause, giving Torino the opportunity to make the transfer permanent at a later date.  This move follows Eray Comert's recent arrival at the club, indicating a clear focus on bolstering their backline.

## Background

Comuzzo is primarily a centre-back who has emerged through Fiorentina's youth system. While he hasn't yet established himself as a regular starter for the Viola, he has shown promise during his appearances and will be hoping to gain valuable experience at Torino. 

Torino, under their current management, have often sought out young talent with potential, looking to develop them within their squad. They are known for a pragmatic, defensively solid approach to the game, so Comuzzo's arrival could provide further stability to their backline. 

## What's next

It is likely that Comuzzo will undergo a medical examination before officially joining Torino.  A formal announcement from the club is expected shortly after these formalities are completed. The buy option clause in the deal suggests that Torino see real potential in the young defender and may well be inclined to make his move permanent if he impresses during his loan spell.
