John Stones is set for a return to Italy after reportedly agreeing terms with Serie A side Inter Milan. The centre-back has reached an agreement in principle on a two-year deal worth €4 million net per season. 

## Background

Stones enjoyed a successful spell at Manchester City, winning numerous trophies including four Premier League titles. He is known for his composed presence at the back and ability to play out from defence.  Inter Milan have been actively pursuing defensive reinforcements this summer as they look to challenge Juventus' dominance in Serie A. The Nerazzurri are renowned for their astute recruitment of talent, often identifying players who can make an immediate impact at a relatively affordable price.

## What's next 

The final details of the transfer are expected to be ironed out shortly, with both parties seemingly optimistic about completing the deal.  Once the agreement is fully formalized, Stones will likely undergo a medical examination before the official announcement is made public.
