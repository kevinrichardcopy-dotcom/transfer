Inter Milan are on the verge of securing a significant defensive addition as John Stones is set to join them as a free agent. While formal procedures still need to be completed, an agreement in principle has been reached between the club and the English centre-back. The deal will see Stones sign a contract that runs until June 2028.

## Background

John Stones is a highly regarded centre-back known for his composure on the ball and ability to start attacks from deep. He's developed into a key player for both club and country, showcasing excellent passing range and tactical awareness alongside his defensive capabilities. Inter, a club with a rich history and passionate fanbase, are renowned for their attacking flair but have often sought reinforcements at the back in recent seasons.

## What's next 

With an agreement in principle already in place, the next steps will likely involve Stones undergoing a medical examination and finalizing the contractual details before an official announcement is made.  Football fans eagerly await confirmation of this potentially impactful transfer, which could significantly bolster Inter's defensive strength for the upcoming season.
