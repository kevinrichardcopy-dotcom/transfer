Paolo Maldini and Leonardo have resigned from their roles at the Italian Football Federation just ten days after joining. The pair stepped down as technical director and advisor respectively, citing disagreements with federation president Gabriele Gravina over the appointment of Andrea Pirlo as head coach. 

## Background

Maldini and Leonardo are considered footballing legends in Italy. Maldini, a stalwart defender for AC Milan during his illustrious career, is renowned for his tactical intelligence and leadership on the pitch. Leonardo, a former winger who also managed clubs like Inter Milan, is known for his attacking flair and experience in coaching.  Their combined expertise made their appointment to the Italian Federation a significant event. However, the federation's internal divisions regarding Pirlo's candidacy appear to have ultimately led to their departure.

Gravina has previously emphasized a desire for stability and experienced leadership within the national team setup. 

## What's next

Given the sudden nature of Maldini and Leonardo's resignation, it is likely that the Italian Football Federation will be forced to swiftly begin the process of finding replacements. This could involve internal appointments or searching for new candidates with a proven track record in managing football teams at high levels. The focus will now shift towards resolving the leadership crisis and establishing a clear vision for the national team moving forward.  It remains to be seen how this situation will impact Italy's upcoming international commitments.
