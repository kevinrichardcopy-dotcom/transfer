Inter Milan are set to discuss a two-year deal with center-back John Stones as a free agent, according to reports. Talks are ongoing between the Italian club and Stones' representatives, though Juventus also approached his camp over the weekend expressing interest. The final decision on his future rests with the English defender, who reportedly also has proposals from clubs in the Premier League.

## Background

John Stones is an experienced center-back known for his composure on the ball and ability to launch attacks from deep. He's spent several seasons at a high level playing for Manchester City, helping them achieve domestic dominance.  Inter Milan are often active in the transfer market, looking to strengthen their squad with established players. Juventus, similarly, have a history of attracting top talent from across Europe. The Premier League clubs interested in Stones likely represent a continuation of his career trajectory at one of England's biggest clubs. 

## What's next

It seems likely that Stones will undergo medical examinations with any club he seriously considers before committing to a deal. An official announcement is expected once the player reaches a final decision and agrees terms with his chosen destination.
