Recent reports linking Xavi Hernandez and Marcelino to the vacant manager's position at Saudi Arabian club Al Ahli appear to be premature. While speculation has been rife in recent weeks, sources close to both coaches indicate that no imminent agreements are in place. 

## Background

Xavi, a legendary midfielder for Barcelona, is currently managing Qatari side Al Sadd, where he has enjoyed considerable success since taking charge in 2019.  He's known for his possession-based, attacking style of play, reminiscent of his days at Camp Nou. Meanwhile, Marcelino boasts extensive experience in La Liga, having managed Valencia, Villarreal, and Athletic Bilbao among other clubs.  

Al Ahli, a prominent club in the Saudi Pro League, is currently searching for a new head coach following the departure of their previous manager. The club has traditionally been known for its ambition and willingness to invest in high-profile players and coaches.


## What's next

It remains to be seen whether Xavi or Marcelino will ultimately take the reins at Al Ahli. If either were to accept the position, it would likely involve a significant move away from their current clubs and environments. It is possible that formal negotiations may begin in the coming days, but for now, these rumors remain just speculation.
