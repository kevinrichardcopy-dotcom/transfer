Leeds United are on the verge of signing Manchester City goalkeeper James Trafford in a deal that could smash their transfer record.  The two clubs have reportedly reached a verbal agreement for a fee of £40 million plus add-ons, with Trafford set to sign a long-term contract at Elland Road. Signatures are expected this week. 

## Background

Trafford has risen through the ranks at Manchester City's academy and is considered one of the most promising young goalkeepers in English football.  While he's yet to make a senior appearance for the reigning Premier League champions, Trafford has impressed on loan spells with other clubs, showcasing his shot-stopping ability and composure under pressure. 

Leeds United are known for their proactive transfer strategy, particularly when it comes to developing young talent. The Yorkshire club recently secured promotion back to the Premier League after a season in the Championship, and they will be looking to bolster their squad ahead of a challenging campaign.  While Leeds has traditionally favoured experienced goalkeepers, the signing of Trafford could signal a shift towards a more youthful approach between the posts.

## What's Next 

The next step in this transfer saga is likely to involve Trafford undergoing a medical examination at Leeds United. If all goes well, an official announcement confirming the move should follow shortly after. The expectation is that Trafford will be integrated into the Leeds first team squad immediately and could even feature in pre-season friendlies before the start of the new Premier League campaign.
